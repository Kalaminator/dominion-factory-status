Why it is better
  • The concept is painted on the real layout, so it always fits the HUD.
  • Parts are cut, not repainted one by one: no drift.
  • Numbers judge the look, not a lenient eye.
  • 2-4 ChatGPT pictures per HUD instead of 17.

Four factories at the same time

Four factories run at once, one faction each, each in its own copy of the code. They share one browser and one test runner, so they take turns for those. When one finishes, its faction joins the game and the factory starts the next faction.
